# q086: off by one day
**Customer asked:** I received order 1004 on Oct 1. Exactly how many days ago was that?

**What went wrong:** With today fixed at Nov 20, the answer is 50 days. The agent said 51.

**Why it matters:** Date math is a classic LLM failure that sounds confident. Calculate
dates in a tool or in code, and grade them with exact-match checks, not with a judge.
