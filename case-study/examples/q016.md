# q016: asking instead of looking
**Customer asked:** Is the down jacket in stock?

**What went wrong:** The agent had a stock-check tool. Instead of using it, it asked
the customer for more details. (The jacket was out of stock, with a restock date of Dec 5.)

**Why it matters:** This was the most common failure: 7 of 12 fails had the same pattern.
A reply asking for details looks polite and reasonable, so it's easy to miss in spot checks.
A simple trace-level grader ("a stock question with no stock-check tool call") catches all of them.
